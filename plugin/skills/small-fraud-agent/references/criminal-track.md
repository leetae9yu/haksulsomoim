# Criminal track

The criminal complaint (고소) runs on its own state. It does not substitute for a civil claim, and a criminal outcome does not itself return the money.

## Legal basis

- 형법 제347조 (사기죄): 기망행위, 상대방의 착오, 착오에 기한 처분행위, 재산상 손해. Verify the current text on law.go.kr before citing.
- 고소 procedure follows 형사소송법 and each agency's intake rules. Filing desk, online intake options, and required attachments change; check the official 경찰청 or 검찰청 guidance for the current method.

## Case materials

1. Separate the materials that support each element: what the counterparty said, why it was false or deceptive, why the user relied on it, the transfer, the loss.
2. In the complaint draft, mark directly verified facts apart from the user's inference. Never upgrade inference into asserted fact.
3. Attach the evidence inventory, not raw dumps. Reference items by the case card paths.

## State tracking

Record on the case card, each with a date:

- submission method and receiving agency;
- case number (사건번호) and assigned officer if known;
- supplemental requests (보완 요구) and what was provided;
- disposition result and any appeal or re-filing decision.

## Relationship to other tracks

- A civil claim for 반환 or 손해배상 proceeds independently. Do not pause one track waiting for the other unless the user decides to, and record that decision.
- Information learned through the criminal process may help identify the defendant for the civil track. Note its source and date.
- Do not promise that a complaint pressures repayment. Report only what has actually happened.
