# Civil and enforcement track

The civil claim and post-judgment enforcement are separate states from each other and from the criminal track. A judgment is not recovery.

## Before filing

1. Fix the claim basis, principal amount, and interest start date. Statutory interest and delay interest follow 민법 and 소송촉진에 관한 특례법; verify the current rate on law.go.kr.
2. Identify the defendant with official-sourced information. Do not assume the transfer account holder is the only possible defendant.
3. Confirm jurisdiction from official sources. Do not assume the nearest court has jurisdiction. Check 민사소송법 venue rules and 소액사건심판법 scope.
4. Choose the procedure with the user: 소액사건심판, 지급명령, or ordinary 소송. Weigh serviceability of the address, likely dispute, and how much defendant information is confirmed. A 지급명령 can fail into ordinary litigation if the debtor objects.

## Filing and trial

1. The user reviews parties, 청구취지, 청구원인, attachments, and every calculation before submission. Filing itself is a user action; see `manual-takeover.md`.
2. Track these as distinct states: 판결 선고, 판결문 송달, 불복기간 경과, 확정. Verify service status against the case record, not assumptions.
3. 무변론판결 is a procedural ruling by the court, not an automatic result. Special service situations, such as service on an incarcerated person, need current rules checked against the case record.
4. When computing enforcement or registry deadlines, do not confuse the oral pronouncement date with the date the 집행권원 became final.

## Enforcement and recovery

Manage each as a separate procedure with its own costs and proofs:

- 재산명시 and 재산조회;
- 채권압류 및 추심명령, including 제3채무자 details and the actual garnish target;
- 강제집행;
- 채무불이행자명부 등재.

Record filing fees, service status, and actual deposits separately. Never record registry entry or obtaining a title of execution as recovery. Report what was actually received, when, and into which account.

## Cost honesty

Total fees, service costs, and expected time belong on the case card before each step. If the realistic recovery is below the expected cost, say so and let the user decide.
